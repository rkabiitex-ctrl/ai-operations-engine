Can I change plans later?

The intended plan structure will allow businesses to upgrade as their needs grow. Plan changes will become available when subscriptions are implemented.

Which currency are prices in?

The proposed public prices are in US dollars (USD), with international billing options to be confirmed before launch.

Can my organisation get a custom plan?

Yes. The Enterprise tier is designed for tailored requirements. A sales contact workflow still needs to be connected.

Are payments enabled now?

Not yet. Payment processing, recurring billing, receipts and subscription management are future implementation steps.
